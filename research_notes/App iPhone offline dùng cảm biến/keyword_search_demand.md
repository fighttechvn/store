## Q1: Which keywords have the highest Google search demand, which are rising fastest (2024→2026), and which are declining?

### Takeaway
On Google web search (US, calibrated to the one public Semrush figure: "qr code scanner" ≈9,900/month), the largest camera-utility queries are "math solver", "image to text", "scanner app", "scan to pdf", "barcode scanner", "measure app", "qr code scanner", "calorie counter" and "cal ai" (each roughly ≥8K US searches/month), plus "translate picture" (8–20K), followed by "document scanner", "lidar scanner", "food scanner", "plant identifier", "photo translate", "photo math" and "night vision camera" (≈3–8K). After discounting a market-wide 2025–26 inflation of "… app" queries (≈+60% baseline), the clearest genuine risers are AI-native, camera-to-structured-data jobs — AI calorie/food/nutrition scanning ("cal ai", "ai calorie tracker", "food scanner", "nutrition scanner"), AI homework ("math solver"), OCR ("ocr app", "text scanner", "scan to pdf", "offline ocr") and LiDAR utilities ("lidar app", "floor plan app", "room scanner", "gaussian splatting") — while plant ID and QR scanning are declining in the US and worldwide (and plant ID in every EU market), and photo/picture translation is declining worldwide and in Vietnam ("dịch bằng camera" −64%).

## Q3: What App Store search popularity and difficulty data exist for these keywords (separate from Google web search)?

### Takeaway
App Store demand must be read separately from Google: Apple's own popularity metric was disrupted in late Sep 2025 (most mid-tail keywords collapsed to the floor value "5"), and public third-party popularity scores exist for only a few head terms. Where numbers are public, document/PDF scanning, "scanner", "QR code reader" and "calorie" are high-popularity (≈61–67 on the 5–100 scale) but very hard (difficulty ≈75–97, top apps with hundreds of thousands to millions of ratings), while identification niches (plant, coin, rock, mushroom, insect) and LiDAR/3D/measure terms show clear App Store query demand in live autocomplete across the US, EU and Vietnam but lack public popularity scores.

### Cited Findings
**How the metric works (and why it is currently unreliable)**
- Apple Ads defines Search Popularity as "the popularity of a keyword, based on App Store searches… displayed as numbers from 1 to 5, with 5 being the most popular" (ASO tools such as Appfigures and ASO.dev publish a finer 5–100 score derived from Apple Ads data) — [Apple Ads Help: Reporting definitions](https://ads.apple.com/app-store/help/reporting/0023-reporting-options-and-definitions) (retrieved 2026-09-27); [MobileAction glossary](https://www.mobileaction.co/glossary/what-is-search-popularity-apple-search-ads/)
- Starting 29 Sep 2025, US App Store keywords with Apple Ads popularity above 5 fell from 165,875 to 39,254 (−77.4%); medium-popularity (20–59) keywords fell from 98,151 to 24,565 and low (10–19) from 66,779 to 10,907; MobileAction, APPlyzer, Appfigures, Astro, Phiture and Radaso confirmed the issue — [ASO.dev blog](https://aso.dev/blog/apple-ads-popularity-massive-drop/)
- Apple's new Monthly Search Term Rank report (introduced Oct 2025) excludes terms with popularity below 35, is updated once a month, offers 1–100 genre-level and overall scales, and has history from July 2024 — [ASO.dev: Search Term Rank](https://aso.dev/aso/search-term-rank/); [ASO.dev blog](https://aso.dev/blog/apple-ads-popularity-massive-drop/)
- ASO.dev now shows the average SAP since 1 Sep 2025 excluding the new "5" values; historical SAP data from Sep 2024 — [ASO.dev keywords docs, updated 28 May 2026](https://aso.dev/aso/keywords/)
- Appfigures said popularity for many keywords "dropped to 5", built another collection method, and keywords updated after 16 Oct (2025) reflect corrected scores; some 5s "appear to be correct" — [Appfigures release notes](https://news.appfigures.com/accurate-keyword-popularity-scores-are-back!-325253)
- Appfigures' Popularity is a 5–100 search-volume score; Competitiveness is 1–100 — [Appfigures keyword teardowns (search-snippet)](https://appfigures.com/resources/keyword-teardowns); [Appfigures KB](https://appfigures.com/support/kb/654/keyword-insights-app-intelligence-for-app-store-optimization)

## Q6: What can and can't Google Search Console provide, and how should it be used once a landing page launches?

### Takeaway
Search Console only reports on properties the user has verified (e.g., the future landing-page domain); it cannot show market-wide search volume for keywords you do not rank for, so it is a post-launch measurement tool, not a pre-launch keyword-research tool. Once the landing page is live and verified, its Performance report (queries, pages, countries, devices; clicks, impressions, CTR, average position) becomes the ground truth for which camera/OCR/LiDAR queries actually surface the page. No GSC data exists for this project yet and none is reported here.

### Cited Findings
- Search Console is a free reporting service for verified site owners; data does not start collecting until ownership of the property is verified — [SEO Stack](https://www.seo-stack.io/blog/why-does-google-search-console-have-a-16-month-data-limit); [Google: About Search Console](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/9128668?hl=en)
- The Performance report shows clicks, impressions, CTR and average position; the default view covers the past three months; dimensions are Queries, Pages, Countries, Devices, Search appearance and Dates; search-type filter covers web, image, video, news; a 24-hour view uses preliminary hourly data; weekly and monthly views exist — [Google Search Console Help: Performance report](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7576553?hl=en) (retrieved 2026-09-27)
- Data grouped by Queries, Countries, Devices or Dates is aggregated by property; data grouped by Pages or Search appearance is aggregated by page, so chart and table totals can differ — [Google Search Console Help](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7576553?hl=en)
- Definitions: clicks = clicks from Google Search to the property; impressions = times a URL appeared in results the user viewed; position = topmost position of the property averaged across queries — [Google: What are impressions, position, and clicks?](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7042828?hl=en)
- "Anonymized queries" (queries not issued by more than a few dozen users over a two-to-three month period) are omitted from query tables but included in chart totals — [Google Search Central Blog, Oct 2022](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2022/10/performance-data-deep-dive)
- The UI exports at most 1,000 rows; the Search Analytics API / Looker Studio connector returns up to 50,000 rows per day per site per search type (API default 1,000 rows; rowLimit up to 25,000 with pagination) — [Google Search Central Blog, Oct 2022](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2022/10/performance-data-deep-dive)
- Retention is a rolling 16 months; older data is deleted unless exported (e.g., bulk export to BigQuery) — [SEO Stack](https://www.seo-stack.io/blog/why-does-google-search-console-have-a-16-month-data-limit); [Google Search Central Community thread](https://support.google.com/webmasters/thread/238677538/gsc-shows-performance-reports-for-the-past-16-month-only?hl=en)
- Data-quality events to know when reading GSC history: Google disabled the &num=100 results parameter around 12–14 Sep 2025, which removed scraper-driven impressions; 87.7% of 319 analysed sites lost impressions and 77.6% lost unique ranking terms — [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-num100-impact-data-462231); [Locomotive](https://locomotive.agency/blog/google-removes-num100-parameter-what-this-means-for-your-website/)
- Google reported that GSC impressions "may have been inflated" between 13 May 2025 and 3 Apr 2026 (fixed 3 Apr 2026); CTR and average position were affected, clicks were not — [SEOPress SEO news, April 2026](https://www.seopress.org/newsroom/seo-news/april-2026/)

### Inferences
- How to use GSC after launch (recommended workflow): (1) verify a Domain property for the landing-page domain (DNS TXT) plus URL-prefix properties per language folder (/de/, /fr/, /es/, /it/, /nl/, /vi/); (2) submit a sitemap; (3) after 2–4 weeks, filter Performance → Queries by regex per cluster (e.g., `scan|scanner|ocr|pdf`, `identif|bestimmen|reconna|identificar|riconosc|herkennen|nhận diện`, `lidar|3d|measure|messen|mesure|medir|misur|meten|đo`) and by Country (DE, GB, FR, ES, IT, NL, US, VN); (4) prioritise queries with high impressions and position 8–20 (page-2 "striking distance") for content improvements, and queries with high impressions but CTR well below the site average for title/meta rewrites; (5) connect the API or BigQuery bulk export from day one to beat the 16-month limit; (6) compare GSC impressions with Google Trends and keyword-tool volumes only directionally, because of anonymized queries and the 2025–2026 impression anomalies.
- GSC will never show App Store search behaviour; App Store queries must be measured in App Store Connect (App Analytics → Sources → App Store Search) and Apple Ads search-term reports.

### Gaps
- No GSC data exists for this project (no verified property); none was fabricated.


## Q7: Could Google Trends be accessed programmatically, how were comparisons made, and what data-quality caveats apply?

### Takeaway
Google Trends was accessible programmatically from this environment (direct calls to the trends.google.com explore/multiline/relatedsearches endpoints with a session cookie; pytrends itself was not installed and the first cookieless call returned HTTP 429). Roughly 300 series were pulled on 2026-09-27 (US, worldwide, Vietnam, UK, Germany, France, Spain, Italy, Netherlands; monthly, Jan 2021–Sep 2026). The data carry a serious 2025–2026 quality caveat: many low-volume and generic "… app" queries show a broad, simultaneous rise from late 2025 and a spike in Mar–Jun 2026 that also appears in unrelated control terms, so growth figures must be read relative to that baseline, not at face value.

### Cited Findings
- Method: each keyword was requested alone (so its own peak = 100) for trend shape, and in batches of five with a fixed anchor ("plant identifier" for English; "dịch ảnh" for Vietnam; the first local keyword for each EU country) for relative level; Sep 2026 is a partial month and was excluded from growth math — [Google Trends explore, e.g. US plant identifier 2021-01-01→2026-09-20](https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?date=2021-01-01%202026-09-20&geo=US&q=plant%20identifier) (retrieved 2026-09-27)
- Growth metrics used: median monthly index Sep 2025–Aug 2026 vs Sep 2023–Aug 2024 ("Δ 2y") and vs Sep 2024–Aug 2025 ("Δ 1y"); a post-spike check (Jul–Aug 2026 vs Jul–Aug 2024); the 2021→2025 change of annual means; seasonality = average month index normalized per year 2021–2025; "spike ratio" = max(Apr–Jun 2026) ÷ median(Sep 2025–Mar 2026) — own computation on [Google Trends](https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?date=2021-01-01%202026-09-20&geo=US&q=document%20scanner) data
- Worldwide controls (Δ 2y): calculator app +109%, compass app +176%, flashlight app +157%, weather app +220%, pdf converter −1%, translate −35%; Germany: calculator app +255%, compass app +433%, weather app +714%, pdf converter +4%, translate −29% — [Google Trends worldwide, weather app](https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?date=2021-01-01%202026-09-20&q=weather%20app)
- Control terms (US, Δ 2y by median): "calculator app" +34%, "compass app" +53%, "flashlight app" +71%, "weather app" +115%, "pdf converter" +14%, "translate" −27%; all "… app" controls bump in Mar–Jun 2026 (e.g., calculator app index 88→100→95 in Mar–May 2026, back to 50–53 by Aug–Sep 2026) — [Google Trends US, calculator app](https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?date=2021-01-01%202026-09-20&geo=US&q=calculator%20app) (retrieved 2026-09-27)
- Example of the spike: US "document scanner" monthly index Aug 2025–Sep 2026 = 29, 29, 27, 33, 32, 38, 40, 53, **100, 93, 98**, 41, 31, 33; "night vision camera" = 14, 14, 13, 17, 15, 13, 20, 26, **100, 61, 61**, 17, 15, 14; "offline translator" = 12, 9, 7, 19, 17, 17, 19, 32, **100, 77, 69**, 20, 10, 10 (Apr–Jun 2026 in bold) — [Google Trends US, document scanner](https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?date=2021-01-01%202026-09-20&geo=US&q=document%20scanner) (each term requested individually)
- An independent analyst (Nick Malekos, 23 Jun 2026) reported Google Trends showing "near-vertical spikes to 100 for terms that have no business moving… Everything is trending" — [B2B Marketing Shots (Substack)](https://b2b.marketingexpertshub.com/p/google-trends-is-broken-why-does)
- Separately, Google confirmed Search Console impressions were inflated 13 May 2025–3 Apr 2026, and scraper-driven impressions fell after the &num=100 removal in Sep 2025 — [SEOPress, Apr 2026](https://www.seopress.org/newsroom/seo-news/april-2026/); [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-num100-impact-data-462231)
- Event contamination: US "lidar" rising queries (last 12 months) are dominated by "Breakout" queries from the video game Arc Raiders ("arc raiders lidar scanner", "lidar scanner control tower", "install lidar scanner on top of galleria sign"), so the Nov 2025 peak in "lidar scanner" is game-driven, not iPhone-LiDAR demand — [Google Trends US related queries, lidar, past 12 months](https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?date=today%2012-m&geo=US&q=lidar)

### Inferences
- Verdict rule used in the tables: a keyword is "rising" only if its Δ 2y exceeds the median of the four "… app" controls by ≥40 points (US baseline ≈ +62%, worldwide ≈ +166%) and "rising fast" if by ≥150 points; "declining" if Δ 2y ≤ −15%; "(spike-inflated)" if the Apr–Jun 2026 spike ratio ≥3×. The inflation is concentrated in "… app" phrasing ("pdf converter" and "translate" did not rise), so non-"app" terms judged against this baseline are, if anything, under-called; treat terms that fell while controls rose (plant identifier, qr code scanner, translate picture, photo translate, dog breed identifier, "translate") as genuinely weakening.
- Head terms with long, stable history (calorie counter, qr code scanner, image to text, handwriting to text, photo translate, plant identifier) are the most reliable trend readings; the brand-new AI terms (ai calorie tracker, calorie counter photo, cal ai, offline ocr, gaussian splatting app) are real emergences (zero before 2024) even after discounting the anomaly.
- Google Trends numbers are relative (0–100 within each request) and cannot be converted to absolute volumes without an external calibration point; see the calibrated estimates table (which depends on a single third-party figure).

### Gaps
- Cause of the 2025–2026 cross-keyword inflation is unconfirmed (AI-assistant "query fan-out", bots, or a Trends sampling change); Google has not (as far as found) published an explanation for Trends.
- Trends cannot provide keyword difficulty or CPC.
